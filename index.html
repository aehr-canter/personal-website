Research

Computational immunology. Designed a multi-epitope Lassa fever vaccine candidate using an immunoinformatics pipeline built on BepiPred 2.0, MHCflurry, AlphaFold, and C-ImmSim. Written up in IEEE format — paper.

Infrastructure ML. Built Water wAIS, a machine learning framework predicting pipe-leak risk across Mumbai's water distribution network, using Random Forest, XGBoost, LSTM, and reinforcement learning against real MCGM data. Submitted to the Stockholm Junior Water Prize (India). There's a working prototype with a Leaflet.js dashboard — demo.

Energy systems. Worked on a thermal sand battery for high-altitude, cold-weather applications during an internship at IIT Bombay, under Prof. Sandeep Kumar (Dept. of Energy Science & Engineering).

I also attended PAIR (Program on AI and Reasoning) in Prague, and completed the Non-Trivial Fellowship, focused on surrogate modelling — Gaussian processes, Kriging, RBF, and neural operators.
